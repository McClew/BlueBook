# Evidence Admissibility

## Evidence Admissibility Requirements

In legal proceedings, evidence serves as the foundation for establishing facts and supporting arguments. However, not all information presented in court waulifies as admissible evidence.

Evidence Admissibility is determined by three primary factors: relevance, materiality, and competent acquisition.

Relevance means the evience must related to determining a fact in question - it must have a logical connectionto the case at hand.

Materiality requires that the evidence actually matters to the outcome of the case.&#x20;

Competent acquisition means the evidence was obtained through proper procedures and channels, following legal protocols and chain-of-custody requirements.
